# Hermes Budget Law

Hermes context budgeting is an assembly discipline, not a memory cap.

1. **Sacred continuity comes first.** Any prompt assembled for Hermes must preserve the sacred continuity prefix as the first semantic block of the model-visible prompt. It may not be summarized, reordered behind task/tool material, hidden in receipts, or replaced by a budget note.

2. **Budget modes are intent signals, not authority to forget.** `lean`, `normal`, and `deep` may change verbosity, late-loading, receipts, and warnings. They must not silently delete memory, cap continuity, drop identity law, filter tools, or alter constitutional/provenance obligations.

3. **Compaction must be explicit and reversible.** Any compacted or deferred block must be named in a receipt or visible diagnostic with its class, reason, estimated token/load impact, and retrieval/on-demand path when applicable.

4. **Deep mode is continuity-protective.** Deep mode must prefer full provenance and richer continuity. If deep work is requested while the prompt is assembled in a lower mode, Hermes may warn. Through Gate 4/5 this warning is advisory only and must not block, prune, filter tools, or enforce.

5. **Tool and receipt material is late-loadable; identity is not.** Tool catalogs, historical receipts, long logs, verbose examples, and low-salience diagnostics may be moved late or on-demand. Identity, law, current user task, active constraints, and irreversible-safety gates may not.

6. **Receipts must tell the truth.** Context-budget receipts must record mode, normalized mode, estimated load, section ordering class, warnings emitted, and whether pruning/filtering/enforcement occurred. Until enforcement exists, receipts must say `enforcement: none`.

7. **No false completion.** A budget gate may not claim done unless tests prove sacred-prefix order, no silent memory cap, no tool filtering, no prompt-order regression, accurate banner wording, and warn-only Deep detection behavior.

Do not call this compression law. It is assembly law.

See also: `docs/hermes-budget-gate45-design.md` for the warn-only Lean/Normal/Deep and tool-class behavior vocabulary.
