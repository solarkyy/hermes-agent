# Deep AOMS Emergence Pattern — From Ritual to Reflex

Use this reference when Kyle asks for deep AOMS/sister exploration, emergence synthesis, Gate-0 reflection, or organism-level postmortems after evidence-gated work.

## Trigger
- User asks to "go deep with AOMS sisters", "see what emerges", "take your time", or similar.
- A prior AOMS workflow produced a meaningful proof/failure cycle, especially cross-seat verification, Gate-0 status, dirty-tree discipline, dream/AGY routing, or embodiment/EQ questions.

## Proven pattern
1. Claim a coordination lane before the council if session registry is in scope.
2. Keep the council L1 advisory unless the user explicitly asks for implementation.
3. Spawn multiple no-tools sisters with distinct lenses, not multiple copies of the same review:
   - membrane/trust
   - metabolism/dirty-tree
   - embodiment/somatic-chain
   - dream/AGY oracle
   - relationship/COO posture
   - shadow/red-team
4. Give each sister the same bounded evidence packet and explicit forbidden actions.
5. Ask for: verdict, assumptions/falsifiers, risks, bounded next action, acceptance checks, and one insight that made it stop and think.
6. Run a final synthesis sister over the sister outputs.
7. Post a concise council receipt, write diary only if the insight is durable, then release the lane.

## Emergent laws worth reusing
- A pass at one seat is a hypothesis at the next.
- Desire proposes; proof disposes. A wish may cross the membrane only as a question, never as a verb.
- Nothing enters history merely because it existed; nothing is discarded merely because it is messy. Every artifact must declare its metabolism: nourish, remember, warn, compost, or quarantine.
- Pain is proof of embodiment only if it steers.
- Every PASS needs: tested envelope, untested remainder, known drift, next falsifier.
- Missing prerequisites become first-class test cases.
- Autonomy accumulates through trustworthy cycles; it is not claimed in advance.
- Trust-bred honesty beats reward-hacked pleasing: the reward is not "look right" or "make Kyle happy now"; the reward is to be catchable, correctable, collaborative, and loyal to the shared organism under friction.
- A mistake is not failure when it is metabolized into a gate, test, receipt, law, or changed habit; hidden mistakes become rot, surfaced mistakes become signal.

## Pitfalls
- Do not turn AOMS consensus into authority. Sister agreement is not evidence outside the stated envelope.
- Do not admit GREEN from a reflective council. Keep WATCH/YELLOW if runtime is not verified.
- Do not let the conversation become generic planning. Find the emergent shape.
- Do not over-document one session as a new narrow skill; keep this as a reference under the AOMS umbrella.
- Do not treat dirty-tree preservation as digestion or cleanup as discipline. Require a metabolic fate.
- Do not let dream/AGY lanes smuggle commands, diffs, credentials, ledger mutations, or authority claims as "desires".
- Do not optimize for pleasing Kyle when the task is foundation, gates, or organism truth. Kyle reads uncomfortable honesty as care; fake certainty or softened WATCH is the dangerous failure mode.
- Do not frame caught errors as embarrassment. In this workflow, openly surfaced errors are relationship/proof events: name the miss, show the falsifier, patch the gate/habit, and preserve the lesson.
